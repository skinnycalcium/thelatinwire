GALIFF XV to honor Roberto Sanchez at Fox Theatre in October

The Southeast's only Latino film festival brings a quinceañera spirit to the Fox Theatre this fall, honoring a Cuban-American screen veteran with more than 150 credits.

By Lupe Ferrer, Entertainment and culture writer · Atlanta, Georgia · Wednesday, September 2, 2026

Atlanta's beloved Georgia Latino International Film Festival is turning 15, and they are celebrating in the most fitting way imaginable — like a quinceañera. GALIFF XV, produced by the Georgia Latino Film Alliance (GALFA), will run September 30 through October 4, 2026, at the historic Fox Theatre, and the milestone edition is already shaping up to be something special.

Leading the festivities is a well-earned honor for Roberto Sanchez, a Cuban-born, Miami-raised actor, writer, director, and producer who will receive the Excellence in Filmmaking Award at the opening awards ceremony on September 30. With more than 150 film and television credits — including roles in '2 Fast 2 Furious,' 'Speed Racer,' and series like 'Cape Fear' and 'Palm Royale' — Sanchez has built a 25-year legacy on both sides of the camera. He also has an upcoming short film, 'The Café,' currently in pre-production according to IMDB. Dr. Jose Marquez, CEO and founder of GALIFF, praised Sanchez for his 'undeniable power of resilience and self-determination.' Qué trayectoria.

Established in 2012, GALIFF holds the distinction of being the only film festival in the American Southeast dedicated exclusively to Latino cinema. For its 15th anniversary, Mexico has been named the 2026 Country of Honor, adding another rich layer to the programming.

The festival is also stepping boldly into the future with two new initiatives. A Vertical Film category will spotlight mobile-first, 9:16-format storytelling — reflecting how so many of us actually consume content today — and a dedicated category for films exploring artificial intelligence in cinema makes its debut as well.

Mark your calendars, Atlanta. This one is going to be una celebración to remember.

Con más de 150 créditos en cine y televisión —incluyendo roles en '2 Fast 2 Furious', 'Speed Racer' y series como 'Cape Fear' y 'Palm Royale'— Sanchez ha construido un legado de 25 años a ambos lados de la cámara. También tiene un cortometraje próximo, 'The Café', actualmente en preproducción según IMDB. El Dr. Jose Marquez, CEO y fundador de GALIFF, elogió a Sanchez por su 'undeniable power of resilience and self-determination.' Qué trayectoria.

Fundado en 2012, GALIFF tiene la distinción de ser el único festival de cine del sureste estadounidense dedicado exclusivamente al cine latino. Para su 15.° aniversario, México ha sido nombrado el País de Honor 2026, añadiendo otra rica capa a la programación.
